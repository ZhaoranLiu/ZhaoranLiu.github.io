About Me
------

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the [Institute for Astronomy, University of Hawaiʻi](https://www.ifa.hawaii.edu), working with [Dr. Rohan Naidu](https://rohannaidu.github.io). My research focuses on the evolution of black holes and galaxies in the early Universe.

Before moving to Honolulu, I was a postdoctoral scholar at the MIT Kavli Institute, working with Dr. Rohan Naidu and [Prof. Robert Simcoe](http://simcoe.mit.edu). I obtained my PhD in Astronomy at the [Astronomical Institute of Tohoku University](https://www.astr.tohoku.ac.jp/en/), supervised by [Prof. Tadayuki Kodama](http://mahalo.galaxy.bindcloud.jp/pg39.html). My doctoral research combined ground-based narrow-band surveys and space-based slitless spectroscopy to study the formation of dust-obscured galaxies.

Outside of research, I enjoy swimming, hiking, and exploring the beaches and trails of Oʻahu.
